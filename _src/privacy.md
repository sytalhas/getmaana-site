Maana: Essential Quranic Words ("Maana", "the app", "we", "us") is published by
{{publisher}} ("the developer"). This policy explains what information the
app handles, why, and the choices you have.

**Summary:** Maana has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Your
learning progress stays on your device. We keep a small amount of data on our
server (hosted by Supabase) to give you an account, record card reports
you send, and manage Premium access.

## 1. Information stored only on your device

The following is stored locally on your phone and is not sent to us:

- Your learning progress: which words you have studied, review schedule,
  streaks, and deck settings.
- Your onboarding answers (for example your chosen pace and intention) and
  simple onboarding step counters used for testing the onboarding flow. These
  counters are kept on the device and are not transmitted.
- App preferences such as theme and reminder time.
- A random identifier generated by the app for card reports (see section 3).

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

## 2. Your account

Maana uses Supabase (supabase.com) for sign-in. Supabase processes this data on
our behalf.

- **Guest (anonymous) account.** On first launch the app creates an anonymous
  account so that you can use it without signing up. This account has a random
  user ID and no name or email.
- **Sign in with Apple.** If you choose it, we receive a user identifier from
  Apple and, if you allow it, your email address (which may be an Apple
  private-relay address).
- **Sign in with Google.** If you choose it, we receive a user identifier and
  your email address from Google.
- **Email and password.** If you choose it, we store your email address and a
  hashed password (handled by Supabase). Supabase sends confirmation and
  password-reset emails.

We use this information only to sign you in, keep your Premium access and your
card reports linked to you, and contact you about your account.

## 3. Card reports

If you report a problem with a word card, the app sends:

- the card's content (Arabic word, transliteration, meanings, example verse
  reference and highlighted word),
- the category you choose and any note or suggested fix you type,
- the app version and platform (iOS or Android),
- your account user ID, if you are signed in (including a guest account), and
- a random device identifier created by the app (not your phone's advertising
  ID or hardware ID). It is used to limit spam and to show you the status of
  reports you sent.

We use reports to correct the word data. Corrections may be published to all
users as content updates. Do not include personal information in report notes.

## 4. Premium, purchases and sponsorships

- **Purchases** (subscriptions, lifetime, and sponsoring another learner) are
  processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive
  your payment card details. The app checks your purchase status with the
  store on your device.
- **Sponsorships.** When you sponsor Premium for other learners, the app sends
  the store transaction ID and platform to our server, linked to your account,
  so the sponsorship can be credited.
- **Free Premium requests.** If you ask for free Premium, we store the request,
  your account user ID, any reason you type (up to 200 characters), and the
  expiry date of the grant. We use this to grant access and to limit how often
  it can be requested.

## 5. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Maana schedules **local** notifications on your
device (for example daily study reminders and a trial-ending reminder). These
are created on the device and are not sent through a push-notification
service. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

## 6. Other network requests

- **Content updates.** The app downloads card corrections from our server.
  This request does not include personal data beyond what is needed to make an
  internet connection.

- **Email links.** Some buttons open your email app with a pre-filled message.
  Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

## 7. What we do not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID).
- No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
- We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.
- The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You can:

- use Maana as a guest without giving an email address;
- turn off notifications;
- ask us for a copy of, or deletion of, your account data by contacting us at
  [{{support_email}}](mailto:{{support_email}}); and
- delete your account from within the app: open the account menu on the home
  screen and choose **Delete account**. This deletes your account and free
  Premium grants and unlinks your reports and sponsorships from you. It does not
  cancel an App Store subscription, which you manage in Settings › Apple ID ›
  Subscriptions. See [how to delete your account](/delete-account/).
