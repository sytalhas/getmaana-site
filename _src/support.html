Common questions

Is Maana really free?

Yes. Every feature of Maana works for everyone: all 910 words, the grammar lessons and the dictionary, with no ads and no trackers. You don’t need an account; the app creates a guest account for you.

What is Maana Supporter, and what are tips?

If you’d like to help keep Maana going, you can optionally support it from the account menu (Support Maana) with a Monthly or Yearly Supporter subscription (auto-renewing), a one-time Lifetime Supporter purchase, or a one-off tip. None of these unlock anything: the only thing a Supporter plan adds is a “Maana Supporter” badge. The price is shown by the App Store in your currency before you confirm.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Supporter subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings › your name (Apple ID) › Subscriptions, choose Maana, and change or cancel your plan. You can also manage subscriptions on the App Store.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app, or deleting your Maana account, does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchases on a new phone?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy. In Maana, open the account menu, tap Support Maana, then Restore purchases. Your Supporter plan and badge will come back. Purchases are checked on your phone with the store; nothing about them is sent to Maana’s server.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I found a mistake in a word. How do I report it?

While reviewing, tap the flag at the top of the word card, choose what’s wrong, and add a note or a suggested fix if you like. We review every report, you can follow its status under My reports in the account menu, and corrections reach everyone as content updates. You can also email {{support_email}} with the word and what should change.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the account menu on the home screen and choose Delete account. This deletes your account and unlinks your word reports from you. It does not cancel a Supporter subscription. More about account deletion, including how to ask by email if you no longer have the app.

What data does Maana collect?

Very little. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking. Your learning progress stays on your phone. Read the Privacy Policy.
