## 1. What Maana is

Maana is an educational app that helps you learn the meanings of frequently
occurring words in the Qur'an, using flashcards, spaced review, audio and
example verses.

Maana is a learning aid. Word meanings, transliterations and grammatical notes
are simplified for learners and may contain errors. They are not a substitute
for a full translation, tafsir, or qualified scholarly guidance. You can report
errors from any card.
