# Demo video script (the owner records it)

One continuous screen recording, about 3 minutes, MP4 under 50 MB (QuickTime > New Screen Recording, then
File > Export As > 1080p). Use the **sandbox** TikTok app and the brand's TikTok account set to private (see
"Before recording the demo" in README.md). Keep the browser's address bar visible the whole time: TikTok checks
that the domain is getmaana.com. Speak or not; if you add captions, keep them short. Show real clicks, no cuts in
the posting part.

## Shot list

1. **Open Studio (0:00 to 0:15).** Browser at `https://getmaana.com/studio/`. Sign in with the team email link.
   The Maana workspace opens.
2. **Connect TikTok (0:15 to 0:45).** Connections > TikTok > Connect. TikTok's consent screen shows the app name and
   the scopes (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish, video.list). Approve. Back in Studio the TikTok card
   shows the connected account.
3. **Open the post (0:45 to 1:00).** Posts > open a carousel draft for TikTok (or Launch > pick a video > tick
   TikTok). Set Method to **Direct post**. (A carousel can only post to TikTok once Studio's photo URL prefix is
   verified, `docs/TIKTOK-PHOTO-VERIFY.md`; until then record this with a video from Launch.)
4. **Creator info and preview (1:00 to 1:15).** Point at "Posting to TikTok as <name> @<username>" and the preview
   of the slides (or the video).
5. **Title and caption (1:15 to 1:30).** Click into the title, change a word; click into the caption, change a word.
   Say or show: the text is ours to edit.
6. **Privacy, no default (1:30 to 1:50).** Show the dropdown reads "Choose who can see this post" and the Post
   button is greyed out. Open it: before the audit the one option is "Only me". Choose it.
7. **Interactions (1:50 to 2:05).** Show all boxes start unticked. Tick Allow comments. For a video, point at a box
   the account has turned off (greyed out with the note).
8. **Commercial content (2:05 to 2:30).** Turn on "Disclose post content": show Post is blocked until a choice.
   Tick Your brand: the "Promotional content" label appears. Tick Branded content: the label changes to "Paid
   partnership", the declaration adds "Branded Content Policy", and "Only me" becomes unavailable with the message
   "Visibility for branded content can't be private". Untick Branded content (unaudited posts must be private),
   leave Your brand on.
9. **Declaration and consent (2:30 to 2:45).** Point at "By posting, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage
   Confirmation" and its link. Click Post to TikTok: the confirmation lists the account, privacy, interactions,
   disclosure, caption and the declaration. Click Post.
10. **Processing and result (2:45 to 3:15).** Studio shows "it may take a few minutes for the post to process and
    appear on the TikTok profile". Wait for the status to change in Posts (Studio polls TikTok's status API), then
    show the phone (or TikTok web) with the post on the private account.

## Second clip (optional, 1 minute)

The same screen for a video from Launch: Duet and Stitch boxes, the duration check, Post.

## Do not show

Secrets, the Supabase dashboard, terminal windows, or any other brand's account.
