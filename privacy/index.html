Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Maana: Essential Quranic Words ("Maana", "the app", "we", "us") is published by Qordova Inc. ("the developer"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Summary: Maana is free, has no ads, and has no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Your learning progress stays on your device. We keep a small amount of data on our server (hosted by Supabase) to give you an account and to record card reports you send. We keep no record of your purchases on our server.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following is stored locally on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Your learning progress: which words you have studied, review schedule, streaks, and deck settings. Progress is not synced to our server or between devices.
  • Your onboarding answers (for example your chosen pace and intention) and simple usage counters (for example which onboarding and Support Maana screens were viewed). These counters are kept on the device and are not transmitted.
  • App preferences such as theme and reminder time.
  • A random identifier generated by the app for card reports (see section 3).

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

2. Your account

Maana uses Supabase (supabase.com) for sign-in. Supabase processes this data on our behalf. Maana has its own Supabase project, separate from any other Qordova app.

  • Guest (anonymous) account. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account so that you can use it without signing up. This account has a random user ID and no name or email.
  • Sign in with Apple. If you choose it, we receive a user identifier from Apple and, if you allow it, your email address (which may be an Apple private-relay address).
  • Sign in with Google. If you choose it, we receive a user identifier and your email address from Google.
  • Email and password. If you choose it, we store your email address and a hashed password (handled by Supabase). Supabase sends confirmation and password-reset emails.

We use this information only to sign you in, keep your card reports linked to you, and contact you about your account.

3. Card reports

If you report a problem with a word card, the app sends:

  • the card's content (Arabic word, transliteration, meanings, example verse reference and highlighted word),
  • the category you choose and any note or suggested fix you type,
  • the platform (iOS or Android),
  • whether you asked to be told when the report is fixed,
  • your account user ID, if you are signed in (including a guest account), and
  • a random device identifier created by the app (not your phone's advertising ID or hardware ID). It is used to limit spam and to show you the status of reports you sent.

We use reports to correct the word data. Corrections may be published to all users as content updates. If you asked to be told, the app shows you when a report you sent has been fixed. Do not include personal information in report notes.

4. Supporter plans and tips

Maana is free, and every feature works without paying. You can optionally support Maana with a Supporter subscription (monthly or yearly), a one-time Lifetime Supporter purchase, or a one-off tip.

  • These purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment card details.
  • The app checks your purchase status with the store on your device, to show the Supporter badge. Nothing about your purchases is sent to or stored on our server.

5. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Maana schedules local notifications on your device (a daily study reminder). These are created on the device and are not sent through a push-notification service. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

6. Other network requests

  • Content updates. The app downloads card corrections from our server. This request does not include personal data beyond what is needed to make an internet connection.
  • Fonts and audio. Fonts and the word and verse audio used in lessons are bundled in the app. The app does not download fonts from Google Fonts or any other service.
  • Email links. Some buttons open your email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

7. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID).
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location, and it does not request microphone, speech-recognition or photo-library permission on iOS or Android.

8. Where data is stored and for how long

Server data is stored by Supabase in the United States (East US, Ohio). We keep account data while your account exists. Card reports are kept for 12 months after they are resolved, and then deleted. If you delete your account, your reports are kept without your user ID or device identifier.

9. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • use Maana as a guest without giving an email address;
  • turn off notifications;
  • ask us for a copy of, or deletion of, your account data by contacting us at support@getmaana.com; and
  • delete your account from within the app: open the account menu on the home screen and choose Delete account (see how to delete your account). This deletes your account and removes your user ID and device identifier from any card reports you sent (the report content is kept so the fix can still be made). It does not cancel a Supporter subscription, which you manage in your App Store account (Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions) or Google Play account.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights.

10. Children

Maana is not directed at children under 13.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
